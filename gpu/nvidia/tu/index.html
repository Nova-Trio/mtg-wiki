NVIDIA Turing drivers

Architecture covers GTX 16xx and RTX 20xx GPUs. Get the firmware from linux-firmware here under nvidia/tu1[??] or from /lib/firmware/nvidia/tu1[??] where [??] is your GPU. The files you are interested in are gsp-535.113.01.bin, bootloader-535.113.01.bin and booter_load-535.113.01.bin. There will be a topic on the 570 driver, but for now 535 works perfectly file. booter_unload-535.113.01.bin is used when unloading, it will appear much later in the article.
